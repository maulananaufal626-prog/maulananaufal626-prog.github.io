🖥️ What Is DesktopLife?

DesktopLife is a charming, lightweight application that transforms your Windows desktop into a lively insect world. It serves two delightful purposes:

  • Screensaver mode: When your computer sits idle, beautiful animated insects like butterflies, ladybugs, and beetles crawl across your monitor.
  • Desktop pet mode: A friendly little fly follows your mouse cursor around as you work, keeping you company without getting in the way.

It's completely free, portable (no installation needed), and works perfectly on both Windows 10 and Windows 11.

🛠️ Requirements

  • Windows 10 or Windows 11 (both 64-bit and 32-bit are supported)
  • Minimum 2GB RAM (4GB recommended for multi-monitor setups)
  • No additional software required – works out of the box thanks to .NET built into Windows
  • Portable storage (USB drive works) for mobility

❓ How To Use DesktopLife

As a screensaver:

  1. Download the zip file from the GitHub page and extract it anywhere.
  2. Right-click the DesktopLife.scr file and select "Install"
  3. Open Windows Settings → Personalization → Lock Screen → Screen Saver Settings
  4. Choose "DesktopLife" from the dropdown and click "Preview"

As a desktop pet:

  1. Simply run DesktopLife.exe
  2. Your pet fly will appear on screen and follow your mouse!
  3. To exit, just close the app from the system tray or press Esc
